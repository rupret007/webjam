Recommended installation
------------------------
1. Drag WebJam.app onto the Applications shortcut in this window.
2. Eject the WebJam disk image, then open WebJam from Applications.
3. If macOS blocks that first launch, open System Settings > Privacy & Security,
   scroll to Security, click Open Anyway for WebJam, and confirm Open.

This app-bundle approval is Apple's supported path for an unidentified app.
Managed Macs may prevent it. Recent macOS versions can block downloaded
`.command` files without offering the same Open Anyway approval, so the
installer helpers below are not the recommended double-click path.

Optional verified installer helpers
-----------------------------------
The two `.command` helpers verify the adjacent app's signature, version, build,
architecture, executable inventory, and transport checksum before installing.
To run one after verifying the release checksum, open Terminal, type
`/bin/bash ` (including the trailing space), drag the chosen helper into the
Terminal window, and press Return.

"Install WebJam.command" preserves quarantine. "Install WebJam - Remove
Quarantine.command" is the advanced path: it displays a warning and removes
Apple's quarantine attribute from the installed WebJam.app only. It does not
disable Gatekeeper globally or change any other application. This is less safe
than Apple's Open Anyway flow.

Neither helper uses sudo. It installs into /Applications when that folder is
writable and otherwise falls back to your personal ~/Applications folder.

First Jamulus sound setup
--------------------------
On a fresh setup, Jamulus may need you to choose your interface, input
channels, headphones, and buffer once for WebJam's dedicated profile. Jamulus
creates and owns those settings. WebJam does not open, read, or change the
regular Jamulus profile.

WebJam keeps its launch workspace, loopback control credentials, and private
Shared Track profile/control files under your own
~/Library/Application Support/WebJam folder. Host, Join, and Shared Track
must not ask WebJam for permission to access data from other apps.

The source retains Music and Podcast & Voice as GA creator profiles. Review &
Rehearsal is Preview. Live WebJam-audio Host/Join, Record Session, and
playback/read-only completed-take review are source-eligible; meanwhile standalone projects, take
edit/comp/mix mutation, track export, shared notes, visual sync, and media
timecode are blocked. No profile directly or automatically taps a meeting app,
browser, or system output. Local scratchpad notes remain profile-scoped on this
Mac only; they are never shared or media-timecoded.

The sealed v0.22.5 Jamulus catalog does not authorize v0.27.2 and is rejected.
The baked registry separately authorizes the existing exact Jamulus 3.12.2 and
3.12.3 records through v0.27.2. Presence of embedded 3.12.2 bytes does not
authorize an identity mismatch. Shared Track uses its separate approved
headless path; unlisted client/server versions still fail closed.

macOS may separately ask for microphone access when you use an audio input.
That expected audio permission is not an Other Application Data prompt. If
macOS says WebJam wants data from other apps, do not add WebJam to Full Disk
Access or treat the prompt as normal. Record the WebJam version and build ID,
close the setup, and report the package as a test-candidate failure.

Optional conversation/video
---------------------------
No meeting service is bundled with WebJam. Any meeting platform can use the
explicit Join / Open Meeting handoff when its link is public HTTPS with a DNS
hostname and passes WebJam's safety checks. Known Webex, Zoom, Teams, Google
Meet, and FaceTime links receive friendly labels; another accepted provider
remains neutral and is not natively verified. WebJam never claims join or
mute. Native verification, installation guidance, mute guidance, and
bring-forward remain Webex-only. Webex is not bundled with WebJam. After
WebJam opens, choose
Conversation on the main session rail or More > Conversation to show the
meeting controls. This does not open or rejoin a meeting. Only Join / Open
Meeting hands off the saved link. Show Webex App dynamically
re-verifies the exact Cisco app and any running PID. It then activates or
launches that verified app itself with no meeting link, document argument, or
browser; Webex chooses its own screen. Open Webex to Mute shows the verified app so
you can use Webex's own Mute control; WebJam cannot verify or change external
Webex mute.
If the native Webex app is missing, Get Webex opens Cisco's official installer
for this Mac after you confirm. Cisco owns that download, license, installation,
sign-in, and its automatic updates. Jamulus remains the music path; keep the
selected meeting service muted while playing to avoid delayed duplicate audio.
WebJam never directly or automatically taps a meeting app, browser, or system
output. Local Originals record explicitly selected input devices, so do not
route meeting or system-output audio into those inputs.
