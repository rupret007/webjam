Check your headphones, then tap Join to start audio.

In the room

    One audio track per person, connected directly where possible. Latency estimates use network RTT/2, received audio buffering, and local audio base latency; device and playback delays may add more.

    A sketchbook for sound

    Headphones are required. Music mode asks for stereo at 48 kHz with echo cancellation, noise suppression, and automatic gain control off. Your browser may choose different settings.

    iPhone and iPad: listen and play along, best effort. Safari/iOS plays mono and can add playback buffering. WebRTC with WebAudio has a history of stuttering (WebKit 225880). Keep this tab visible and tap Resume audio if playback pauses.

    Firefox: the browser may send a lower audio bitrate.

    Free service limits apply. A busy relay pauses or refuses new connections. Joining several people from one network may take a few minutes. No account, analytics, or paid fallback.
