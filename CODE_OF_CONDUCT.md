# Code of Conduct

WebJam is a collaborative project. Participants are expected to be respectful,
specific, patient, and constructive. Make room for different musical,
technical, cultural, and accessibility needs.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing,
deliberate exposure of credentials or private meeting data, and disruption of
another contributor's work.

If a problem occurs, contact the repository owner privately through GitHub.
Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may remove
content, pause participation, or close contributions when necessary to protect
people and the project.
